    Who

    Dr Non — Bangkok, 1981 →

    That’s me. Days old. Siam Square — the most expensive ground in Bangkok today. Back then it was just where our house was. I grew up in the heart of this city and learned it from the inside: heat, floods, skytrain openings, AQI at 200, tax schedules frozen while the market doubled.

    I am not going to let anyone fuck up this city. Not the current governors. Not the deputies. Not the committees writing ten-year “smart city” PDFs while the public gets nothing they can use. No apologies. I’m building something — a working twin of the city I was born into. My city. Not theirs.

    Thesis

    One laptop. Zero politicians who care.

    The BKKx Atlas is a thesis statement: a working digital twin of a real city can be built in an afternoon, on one laptop, for the cost of a domain. The data, APIs, and maps are here. What’s missing is a city government with the will to use them.

    BMA has been “planning a smart city” since 2016. Ten years. Ten digital strategies. Zero public APIs worth using, zero digital twin for residents. Buildings get taller. Floods get deeper. The public still gets a 1990s PDF. We shipped the whole stack on a one-laptop budget. The hard part is keeping every layer honest and stackable.

    How it’s built

    Basic infrastructure — still one machine

    • Front. Static MapLibre GL atlas in public/ — no SPA framework. Layers, presets, inspect, and this About drawer are plain HTML / CSS / JS.
    • Edge. Cloudflare Worker (src/index.ts) on atlas.nonarkara.org: proxies live feeds, serves risk / civic APIs, Range-passthrough for PMTiles from R2.
    • Tiles & store. 432,077 OSM buildings as PMTiles on R2; civic amenities in D1 (CIVIC_DB) ingested from BMA / data.go.th.
    • Live inputs. iTIC/Longdo traffic + CCTV, RainViewer radar, Open-Meteo heat/AQI, HII/ThaiWater flood gauges — honest about what is live vs schematic.
    • Reuse. GeoJSON downloads and /api for Chula, UNL, or anyone building on Bangkok without a consulting fee.

    What’s on the map

    Shipped — stackable, honest

    1. Multi-layer city stack. Buildings under translucent rain, heat, AQI, and flood. Presets: City · rain · AQI · heat · Flood watch · Night · Full pulse.
    2. Inspect + risk API. Click a building → OSM height/type + schematic flood × live PM2.5 via /api/risk.
    3. Live traffic + CCTV. iTIC/Longdo expressways and public road cameras (HLS). Not the closed BMA security net. /api/cctv
    4. Civic open data. Amenities + density by district from BMA / data.go.th via D1 — /api/civic-pois.
    5. Land price by district. Real Treasury road-level analysis as a choropleth — not a fake heat paint.
    6. Flood sensors + rain radar. HII/ThaiWater gauges and animated RainViewer frames over the silhouette.
    7. Night lights / blackout. Desaturated satellite + emissive buildings — pure client paint.

    Roadmap

    Still one laptop

    1. Official flood extents. Swap the schematic envelope for DDPM/BMA GeoJSON when it’s open.
    2. Property Tax Verifier (ภาษีที่ดิน) v2. Deed / parcel lookup over Treasury zones vs a typed tax bill.
    3. BTS/MRT isochrones. 5 / 10 / 15-minute walksheds from every rail station.
    4. Risk API v3. Fold rain intensity + building height into the score.
    5. Offline atlas pack. Pin MapLibre + pmtiles + samples onto a USB stick.

    Horizon

    1. BKKx Digital Twin — handover to BMA. Open-source, no lock-in, no ฿5.4M consulting fee.
    2. Property-tech API (full). 432K buildings + cadastral polygons — institutional, not code, is the blocker.
    3. Tourist AR · planning sandbox · command center. Same Worker, denser chrome — after the civic layers are boringly reliable.
